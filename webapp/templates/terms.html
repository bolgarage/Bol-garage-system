4. Reservations and payments

  • The reservation fee is at least ₱5,000. The exact amount and holding period are agreed with the buyer, usually for one day.

  • Accepted payment methods are cash, bank transfer, and GCash. Always get a written acknowledgment or receipt for any payment.
  • Pay only to accounts under Bol Garage’s confirmed details. We will never ask you to pay to a personal account that was not confirmed by us in person or by phone.

5. Documents and transfer of ownership

  • Every sale is documented with a Deed of Sale and the vehicle’s OR/CR. Both parties sign, and we recommend notarization.
  • Bol Garage can assist with LTO transfer and registration renewal for a reasonable fee, which will be discussed before the service. Buyers may also handle the process themselves. Registration fees, taxes, and transfer costs are for the buyer’s account unless agreed otherwise.
  • The buyer must present a valid government ID.

6. Buying vehicles from sellers

  • We only buy vehicles that the seller legally owns, with complete and authentic documents. Vehicles with liens, unpaid loans, or unclear ownership will be declined.
  • The seller must provide the original OR/CR and a valid ID, and must sign the Deed of Sale.
  • The seller confirms the vehicle is not stolen, not subject to a pending case, and free of undisclosed encumbrances. Suspected stolen vehicles may be reported to the authorities.
  • Our offer is based on inspection and market value and may change if we find issues that were not disclosed.
