Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

Bol Garage (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile number, email address, and Facebook/Messenger name when you message or inquire about a vehicle or part.
  • Transaction details: for buyers and sellers, a valid government ID, address, and vehicle documents (OR/CR, deed of sale, plate and engine/chassis numbers) needed to complete a sale or transfer of ownership.
  • Payment details: records of reservation fees, down payments, and balances paid. We do not store card or bank login details.
  • Website/Facebook data: basic technical information needed to load and secure the website, and messages you send to our official Facebook page.

2. Why we collect it

  • To answer questions and schedule viewings or test drives.
  • To verify identity and ownership and prepare sales documents.
  • To process payments and issue receipts.
  • To help with ownership transfer and follow-up after a sale.
  • To comply with legal requirements and help prevent fraud and carnapping.

3. How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed with:

  • Government offices such as the LTO, and notaries, to complete a transfer of ownership.
  • Financing or insurance partners, only if you ask us to process an application.
  • Authorities, when required by law or a lawful order.

4. How long we keep it

We keep transaction records only as long as needed for the sale, after-sale concerns, and legal or tax purposes, then delete or securely dispose of them.

5. How we protect it

We keep physical and digital records in limited-access storage, and only authorized people handle them.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access or correct your personal information, object to its use, or request deletion or withdrawal of consent. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

7. Photos and posts

Photos of vehicles for sale may be posted on our Facebook page and website. We will not post photos of a buyer or seller, or show IDs, plate numbers of customers’ own vehicles, or personal details, without permission.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version and effective date will always be shown on this page.
